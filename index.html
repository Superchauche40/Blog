A little about the writer

Behind the Diaries

I'm a storyteller and pop-culture enthusiast who has always found something fascinating in the worlds people create. Movies, television, comics, video games, music, and the people behind them have all shaped the way I look at storytelling.

I'm also the creator behind Singerverse, a multimedia storytelling universe. The ideas, characters, and experiences that come from creating it are part of a much bigger creative journey.

This journal is where I can explore those connections: what works, what doesn't, what inspires me, and what I take away from the stories I experience. Sometimes it'll be criticism. Sometimes it'll be appreciation. Sometimes it'll just be me thinking out loud.

The goal isn't to have the final word on pop culture. It's to have a conversation with it.
