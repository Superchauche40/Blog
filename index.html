Behind the Projector

Every story leaves something behind.

I'm a storyteller and lifelong pop culture enthusiast. This journal is where I explore the movies, shows, comics, games, music, and ideas that catch my attention.

It's also a record of the connection between experiencing stories and creating them. What I discover here may find its way into the worlds I build, the characters I write, and whatever comes next.

This isn't a review site with a score for everything. It's a place for reactions, questions, observations, experiments, and the occasional rabbit hole. Consider this your ticket to the show.
