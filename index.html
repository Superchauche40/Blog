About the Journal

Welcome to Drive-In and Diaries, a personal journal dedicated to the stories that entertain us, inspire us, and stay with us.

From movies and television to comics, video games, and music, this is a place to share thoughts, experiences, discoveries, and the creative ideas that come from enjoying the things we love.

This isn't just about reviewing something and moving on. It's about looking at the stories themselves, the people behind them, and the ways they influence our own creativity.

Sometimes that means discussing a favorite character. Sometimes it means sharing an original story. And sometimes it simply means writing down a thought worth remembering.
